# PRIME: Pitch Perfect

Pitch Perfect is a meeting notebook for any business, made by Prime Digital Connect. Set up your company once: what you sell, your prices, your real results, your colors and your logo. Then give it a prospect's name and website. It researches them and builds:

- a deck you can click through
- a talk track
- a cheat sheet
- a leave-behind

It also makes complete ad campaigns and brand kits, and it keeps your clients, follow-ups and notes on your own computer.

The current version is 0.31.0. iPhone, iPad and Android are coming as a web app.

Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu and choose **About This Mac**. If it says "Chip: Apple M…", download Apple silicon. If it says "Processor: Intel", download Intel.

## Install

**Windows:** Open the file. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** Open the disk image and drag the app to Applications. The first time you open it, right-click the app, choose **Open**, then choose **Open** again. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the Mac asks once.

## Before your first kit

Research needs an Anthropic API key, the kind that starts with `sk-ant-api03-`. Create one at console.anthropic.com, then paste it into the app under **Settings → Research key**. The key is stored encrypted on your computer and is only used to run your research.

## Where your work lives

Everything is saved in `Documents/PRIME Pitch Perfect`: kits, clients, ads and brand kits. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.
