# PRIME: Pitch Perfect

Pitch Perfect is the meeting notebook from Prime Digital Connect, for Windows. Give it a prospect's name and website and it researches them. Then it builds everything you need for the meeting:

- a growth deck you can click through
- a talk track
- a cheat sheet
- a leave-behind

It also keeps your clients, follow-ups and notes on your own computer.

## Download

**[Download PRIME: Pitch Perfect for Windows](https://github.com/afieldremains/pitch-perfect/raw/main/PRIME-Pitch-Perfect-Setup.exe)** (version 0.29.0, about 100 MB)

The download starts as soon as you click. SHA-256: `e428481b592ef9a327959783021c13612d390c28d1c8c84ac8b599902e76cbdd`

## Install

1. Open the file you downloaded.
2. Windows may show a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC". The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognize it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. The app opens when it finishes.

## Before your first kit

The research needs an Anthropic API key, the kind that starts with `sk-ant-api03-`. Create one at console.anthropic.com, then paste it into the app under **Settings → Research key**. The key is stored encrypted on your computer and never leaves it except to run your research.

## Where your work lives

Kits and clients are saved in `Documents\PRIME Pitch Perfect`. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.
